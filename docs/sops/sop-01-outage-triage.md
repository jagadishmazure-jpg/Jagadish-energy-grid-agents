# SOP-01 — Outage Triage

## 1. Intake
Log every outage report with feeder, customers affected, cause code, and
start time. Reports from field sensors outrank customer calls for
confirmation.

## 2. Severity scoring
Score each outage as customers × critical-facility factor × restoration-time
factor. Critical facilities (hospitals, water plants, fire stations) double
the score. Anything scoring critical pages the duty officer immediately.

## 3. Tiers
- T1 (critical): duty officer paged, crew dispatched within 30 minutes.
- T2 (high): dispatch within 2 hours, storm desk notified if weather-related.
- T3 (medium): next available crew, batch into restoration waves.
- T4 (low): schedule within the business day.

## 4. Communication
Publish estimated restoration times to the outage map only after a crew
confirms the cause on site. Never publish an ETR before confirmation.
