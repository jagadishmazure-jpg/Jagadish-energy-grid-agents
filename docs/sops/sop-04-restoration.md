# SOP-04 — Restoration

## 1. Order of restoration
Restore in this order: critical facilities first, then the largest
customer blocks, then the remainder. Document any deviation with a reason.

## 2. Verification
A crew must confirm the fault is cleared and the feeder tests clean before
re-energizing. Re-energize in stages for feeders above 5,000 customers.

## 3. Closure
Close the outage only after telemetry shows stable load for 15 minutes.
Record cause, crew, restoration time, and customers affected in the outage
log for the after-action review.

## 4. After-action
Every T1 outage gets an after-action review within 5 business days.
