# SOP-02 — Crew Dispatch

## 1. Eligibility
Only crews with status "available" may be dispatched. Committed crews are
never pulled off an active job. Night shift covers critical and high
severity only.

## 2. Scoring
Score eligible crews on proximity (45%), skill match (35%), and shift/SLA
fit (20%). Required skills come from the outage cause: overhead lines need
overhead certification, switching operations need switching certification.

## 3. Assignment
Assign the highest-scoring crew and record the full scoring breakdown in
the audit log. If no crew qualifies, escalate to the duty officer instead
of assigning an unqualified crew.

## 4. Mutual aid
When every internal crew is committed during a major event, the duty
officer may request mutual aid from neighboring utilities. Mutual-aid
requests always need human confirmation.
